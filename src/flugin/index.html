The short version

What an engagement looks like

01

Assess

We map your recurring work, covering the deliverables, the monthly grind, the places where hours leak, whether that is a client roster or a household. No build begins before the map is validated.

02

Deploy

Choose managed and you are live in days on our cloud. Choose sovereign and a field unit arrives configured for your track, joining your network from day one. Either way, the same gate governs everything.

03

Activate

The grid takes over preparation work under your pack's rules. You review and commit through the Surface. Adoption is measured, not assumed.

04

Steward

We operate, patch, and improve the system under a simple retainer. You can leave anytime and take everything with you.
